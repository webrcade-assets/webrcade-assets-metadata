Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood follows the adventures of the Elric brothers, Edward and Alphonse, as they seek the Philosopher's Stone to restore their bodies after a failed alchemical experiment. The game's story mirrors the events of the "Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood" anime series. Gameplay involves high-speed 3D action and beat 'em up mechanics. Players can form tag teams with characters like Alphonse and Roy Mustang, utilizing physical combat and alchemy. Understanding the environment is crucial as alchemy relies on available materials.
