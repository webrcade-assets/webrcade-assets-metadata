EyePet Adventures is the second game in the EyePet series for the PSP. The basic concept is the same: by pointing the PSP with the PSP camera attached on the 'magic card' the player can interact with a cute pet - play mini-games, feed and wash it, customize its appearance. The added features include the new Explorer Club mode - the player controls a submarine-like transport (called EyePet Explorer Craft) with the pet inside exploring the 2D-scrolling environments for treasure and customization items. The creature's new home and customization area is located inside this EyePet Explorer Craft.
