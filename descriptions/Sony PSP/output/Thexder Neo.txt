During a routine survey, the interstellar weapons platform Laevina detects Nedium, an asteroid of unknown origin, and soon finds itself in the grip of the planetoid's ultramagnetic field. Shipwrecked atop an impregnable fortress of rock, the starship turns to its last resort—the Hyper Dual Armor prototype Thexder. Based on the 1985 classic, Thexder is a side-scrolling shooter featuring sleek animation, strategic transforming-mech gameplay, and a homing laser attack. Now the excitement of the original returns with revamped graphics, a novice mode, 6-player online races, and all-new features in Thexder Neo.
