Taito Legends series of compilations moved onto handheld in Europe and the US with Taito Legends: Power-Up. It is slightly different from Taito's own Taito Memories Pocket, which was only released in Japan. Most notable is the inclusion of Space Invaders and Space Invaders Part 2 and the absence of Rainbow Islands Extra. It contains 21 games and 4 additional remakes which were included in Taito Memories Pocket: Alpine Ski Balloon Bomber Cameltry Chack'n Pop Crazy Balloon Elevator Action The Fairyland Story KiKi KaiKai Kuri Kinton The Legend of Kage Lunar Rescue New Zealand Story Phoenix Qix Raimais Rastan Saga Return of the Invaders Space Chaser Space Dungeon Space Invaders Space Invaders Part 2
