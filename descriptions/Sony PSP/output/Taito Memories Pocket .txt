A compilation of Taito's retro games comes to the handheld market in Taito Memories Pocket. All the games have some changes like the total number of levels in Carry Balloon has been increased from 50 to 100 and special items that make the balloon small, fast, slow, and invincible. 20 games in all, 16 retro games from the 80s and 4 remakes:     Crazy Balloon + Crazy Balloon 2005     Balloon Bomber + Balloon Bomber 2005     Lunar Rescue     Qix     Alpine Ski     Elevator Action     Chack n' Pop     Legend of Kage + Legend of Kage 2005     Fairyland Story     Kiki Kaikai Ninja     Rastan Saga     Kuri Kinton     New Zealand Story     Ramais     Rainbow Islands Extra     Cameltry + Cameltry 2005
