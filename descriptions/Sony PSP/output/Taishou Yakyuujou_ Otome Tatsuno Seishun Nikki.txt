The Visual novel based on Jun Kagurazaka's original work is now available as a game! Under the supervision of the original author, various stories develop depending on the choices in the original story, making it a specification that can be enjoyed any number of times. Fully voiced by the same cast as the anime version! You can enjoy realistic conversations between characters. And the game original theme song and BGM are also newly recorded. The time is the 14th year of Taisho, July- Koume, the only daughter of the western restauranteur , is 14 years old and attends Toho Seika Girls' High School. At a school attended by children from good families, Akiko, a good friend of mine, suddenly "I want you to play baseball with me!" Koume nodded for some reason, but there were so many things he didn't know, such as whether the nine of them would be together, what tools they would use and how they would be used, and what the rules were... Can girls beat boys in baseball? A story of the struggle of maidens in the Taisho era who want to blow a bubble in the male-dominated world!
