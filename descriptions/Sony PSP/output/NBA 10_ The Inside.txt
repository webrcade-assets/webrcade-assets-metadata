NBA 10: The Inside is a basketball simulation game exclusive to the PSP and the follow-up to NBA 09: The Inside as an entry to Sony's NBA franchise. Along with the standard career and franchise modes that emphasize realism, the game includes a variety of mini-games such as hot shot, whack-a-mole and skee ball. The game also includes the Conquest mode that first appeared in NBA 09: The Inside, as a Risk-like board game, based on the mechanics of mini-games such as Dodgeball, Elimination and Give & Go. By winning games an 'army' of basketball players challenges NBA teams all over the country by visiting their cities and players can also be lost to other teams.
