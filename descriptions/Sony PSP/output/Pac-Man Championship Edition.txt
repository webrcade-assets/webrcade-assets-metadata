In "Pac-Man Championship Edition," players control the iconic character Pac-Man as he navigates through mazes, eating pellets and avoiding ghosts. The game retains the classic gameplay mechanics of the original "Pac-Man" but introduces new elements such as time-limited gameplay and dynamically changing mazes. As players progress, the maze layout alters, requiring quick reflexes and strategic movement to maximize scores. The game features various modes, each with different objectives and challenges. Players can compete for high scores in time attack modes or complete specific tasks in challenge modes. The visual style is updated with vibrant colors and enhanced graphics, while the core mechanics remain true to the original arcade classic.
