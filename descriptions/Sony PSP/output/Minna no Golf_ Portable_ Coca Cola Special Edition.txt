This is a specially Coca Cola branded version of Hot Shots Golf Open Tee. The game is the same except that there is a Coca Cola theme to everything. The characters wear Coke's red-and-white colors, there are Coke branded billboards, Coke "thought bubbles", even Coke bottle-shaped golf clubs and the game comes in a Coke themed package. This was given away in a Coke Style special giveaway with a Coca Cola branded PlayStation Portable. Only 1300 copies of this game were made.
