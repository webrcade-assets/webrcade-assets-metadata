World Tour Soccer 2 is the follow up to the PSP game World Tour Soccer. Gameplay is based on its predecessor, you have the ability to enter a team from the leagues of various countries in their domestic leagues and tournaments or international teams in the world cup and their respective continental cups. The player will also be able to play these teams in various point based mini games that award points for better gameplay including Classic Challenge (in which you earn points for successful tackles, passes, goals scored ETC and loose points for bad tackles, incomplete passes and so on), All Rounder (passing the ball to all eleven players on your team before goals can be scored, any before are not counted), Time attack (scoring before the timer reaches zero) as well as several others. There is also the World Tour Mode which allows players to take on various challenges on which they can earn bronze, silver and gold medals on, the better players do, the more challenges that can be unlocked and played. Players can also play multiplayer games over the internet (via infrastructure support) or locally via the PSP's ad-hoc support with up to eight other players. players can play one on one with other players or you can team up with three friends to take on another team of four.
