Comic Party Portable is a non-adult port of Comic Party, an adult visual novel for PC (Windows). While there may not have been and new additions to the story, the PSP port features full voice-acting, which was later included in the PC re-release DCE version, while the original PC release had only partial voice-acting. New heroine present in the Dreamcast port is also included in the PSP game release. While the opening video features the same video that Dreamcast and PC's DCE version used, which was taken from the "Comic Party: Revolution" OVA series, the PSP port uses new song over it.
