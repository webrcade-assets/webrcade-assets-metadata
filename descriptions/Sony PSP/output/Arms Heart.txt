Forget about justice, rules and ideals, just keep your objectives and greed in mind. As a key member of the underground organization "The Lamb of Johann" you are going to pit yourself against other ambitious villains to get what you want.Unlike other turn base RPGs, there is an action aspect to the battles. Get used to the Howling system and get your attacks timed right to release your full power. When you kill monsters, you'll win items for alchemy. If you win a key item, you can even play with Hazard Alchemy to make your homunculus grow and learn new skills.
