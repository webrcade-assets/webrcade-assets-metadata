Eloquent Fist is a 2D side-scrolling beat 'em up based on the Ikkitōsen high school manga franchise created by Yuji Shiozaki. The gameplay combines typical beat-'em-up gameplay fighting multiple enemies and many cut-scenes between the fights that further the story about turf wars between rival schools. In the main Story mode one of three plotlines is followed, each following different schools and often switching the playable character between scenes. The characters' health and special meters can be upgraded. In the Arcade mode you pick one character and fight through seven stages.
