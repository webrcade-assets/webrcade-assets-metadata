Kurohyō: Ryū ga Gotoku Shinshō is a portable spin-off of the main Ryū ga Gotoku series developed for the PSP by Sega and Syn Sophia. The game does not follow the established series canon and is a side story focusing on high school gang member Tatsuya. Despite the story shift the game still takes place in the series' iconic city of Kamurocho. The game was only released in Japan.
