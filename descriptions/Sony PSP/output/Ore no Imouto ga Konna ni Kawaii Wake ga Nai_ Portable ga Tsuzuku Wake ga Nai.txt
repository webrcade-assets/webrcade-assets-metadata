Ore no Imoto ga Konnani Kawaii Wake ga Nai Portable ga Tsuzuku Wake ga Nai, lit. "There's no Way 'My Little Sister Can't Be This Cute Portable' Could Continue"), shortened as Ore no Imouto P-Zoku, is the sequel to the PSP game Ore no Imouto ga Konnani Kawaii Wake ga Nai Portable, both of which are developed by Bandai Namco Games. Ore no Imouto P-Zoku retells the events of the Ore no Imouto ga Konna ni Kawaii Wake ga Nai light novels in visual novel format with new story elements added. It was released on May 17, 2012. The game will include two discs, the first one being an updated version of the first game, and a second disc with additional elements added in. Data from the original game can be transferred to this game's first disc, saving the trouble of having to unlock everything the player has unlocked before.
