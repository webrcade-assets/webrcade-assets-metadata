Create and raise your characters to defeat the monsters that lurk deep in the labyrinth-like dungeons. Its simple gameplay and sense of freedom makes you feel like you are truly inside a fantasy world. Choose from 16 occupation including the likes of Fighter, Mage and Samurai. With 9 races to choose from, diverse parties can be created set according to gender, age and their personality. In addition, customize the game to your liking with the graphic/sound functions. With many events along the way, fulfill the main quest to search for the 5 rings. Let's open the gates and head out to a new adventure.
