THE KING OF FIGHTERS '94, the first game hailed as a dream match of SNK characters, makes its way to the NEOGEO STATION! Still beloved by countless fans, its revolutionary three-on-three team battle system sets it apart from all other fighting games. And of course, this NEOGEO STATION port allows you to test your skills against another player through an Ad Hoc connection in Network Mode, record your local gameplay with Replay Mode, and listen to game music in the SOUND PLAYER for even more fun! The Ad Hoc functionality of this software will not work on PSP®1000 systems.
