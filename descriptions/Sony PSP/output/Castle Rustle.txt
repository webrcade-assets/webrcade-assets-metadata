Castle Rustle is a merry and kind game for the real candy-lovers! The Candyland is in peril! An evil magician is about to invade the Candyland and add it to his Dark Empire. The army of enchanted monsters is near and they are ready to chew and swallow everything in their wake! However, there is still hope as the brave people of the Candyland are determined to defend their beautiful caramel castles, marshmallow towers and chocolate forests.
