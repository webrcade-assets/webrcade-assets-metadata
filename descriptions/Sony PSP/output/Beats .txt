Beats is a customizable rhythm & music game. There are two basic modes of gameplay: Music challenge and Jamming. In Music challenge the player needs to press buttons corresponding to what appears on the screen with proper timing to score points while listening to music. Jamming mode lets the player to compose his own tracks with a variety of preset musical instruments and loops. Jamming sessions can be saved and shared online. The game uses its own music tracks as well as user’s MP3 music collection. Multiplayer is available for up to 4 players via Ad Hoc.
