Phi Brain: Kizuna no Puzzle is an action puzzle game, developed and published by Arc System Works, which was released in Japan in 2012. The player is required to solve the puzzles via the playable main character - Daimon Kaito. At the last of each stage, a 3D reproduced POG puzzle will appear. Various puzzles are inspired by the anime and by collaborating with other characters in the game, the player is also able to improve the friendship. There are also exclusive contents available in the "Archive Mode" based on the player's performance. In addition, depending on the progress of the puzzle, you can enjoy a higher degree of puzzle difficulty via the "Story Mode".
