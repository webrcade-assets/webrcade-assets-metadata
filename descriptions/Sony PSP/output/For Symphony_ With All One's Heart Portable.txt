Originally for Dreamcast platform, this game was soon ported to Windows with several updates. However, PlayStation 2 release features yet more additional updates which include full voice-acting, renewed videos and art, new CG events and 2 new characters. The game story, unlike in many visual novels and love simulations, let you select a male or female student to play as. Most of the events take place in high-school during your senior year. The story and love interest will change depending whether you play as a boy or a girl. During the conversation, you are occasionally presented with the choice selection that decide the direction the remaining story take.
