SD Gundam G Generation Overworld is a tactical role-playing game where players command various Gundam units and pilots from different Gundam series. The storyline involves managing a fleet and leading them through various missions set in the Gundam universe. The game features a strategic grid-based combat system, allowing players to upgrade units, complete missions, and unlock powerful abilities. Players can participate in 'Break missions' and 'Challenge missions' that trigger special events and unlock new characters. The game also includes various modes and the ability to develop units through experience points gained in battles.
