Duke Nukem: Critical Mass is an unreleased PSP shooter by Frontline Studios, in 2022 Unseen64 & ObscureGamers both released the data although the Unseen64 copy had correct file dates, a third copy was sent to HiddenPalace that matched the data ObscureGamers had been sent (Which was incomplete & had edited file dates). The build was created June 30, 2010. Just a tip, the build requires a PSP with 64MB ram to function, and PPSSPP v1.1.1 to emulate.
