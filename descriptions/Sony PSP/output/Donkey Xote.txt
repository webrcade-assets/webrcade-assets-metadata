Donkey Xote is an adventure game where you play as the main character Don Quixote taken from the animated comedy adventure movie published in 2007. The movie tells the story about a slightly crazy Don Quixote, but actually he is is very clever, smart and very close to his horse friend Rosinante. Later on... competition between Don Quixote and Panza causes them to do a journey across Spain and Barcelona to get the heart of a beautiful woman called Dulcinea.
