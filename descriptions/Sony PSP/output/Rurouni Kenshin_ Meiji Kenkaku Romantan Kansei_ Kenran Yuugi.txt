Rurouni Kenshin: Meiji Kenkaku Romantan Kansei - Kenran Yuugi (るろうに剣心 -明治剣客浪漫譚- 再閃 絢爛遊戯) is a Puzzle video game published by Bandai Namco Games released on August 25th, 2013 for the PlayStation Portable. This is great, a Rurouni Kenshin inspired version of arkanoid. It’s pretty fun, you not only move but can time a sword hit on the ball to generate extra hits.
