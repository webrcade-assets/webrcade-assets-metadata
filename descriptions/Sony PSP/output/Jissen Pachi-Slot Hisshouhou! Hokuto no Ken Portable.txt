Previously released on the Nintendo DS, Jissen Pachislot Hisshouhou! Hokuto no Ken is a hybrid game mixing pachi-slot (pachinko/slot machine) action with the ultraviolent apocalyptic world of Hokuto no Ken (AKA Fist of the North Star). This game features an animated representation of Ken wandering the futuristic streets. When battles occur, decisions and events are decided not fighting game mechanics, but by gambling action. Expect a story mode and plenty of (gambling-operated) punches and kicks in the familiar Hokuto no Ken tradition.
