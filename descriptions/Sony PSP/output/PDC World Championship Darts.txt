PDC World Championship Darts is an officially licensed darts game. The player can opt to play as champions such as Allan Warriner-Little, Raymond van Barneveld or Phil Taylor or create his own player. Unlike older versions in the series, this edition features on-line exhibition multi-player modes both on the Xbox 360 through LIVE and on the PSP through wireless play. Additionally the PSP version allows the import of customized photos and music playlist through the Memory Stick Duo.
