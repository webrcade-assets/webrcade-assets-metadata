SBK-07: Superbike World Championship is the official game based on the Superbike World Championship. All the real elements are in the game, including official tracks, riders, and sessions. The player may take part in quick races, practice session, time attacks, full championships, multiplayer matches, or challenges. Additionally, the player may also customize the riding experience by activating or deactivating a large amount of riding helps. There are 22 bikes in the game and different racing modes, from straightforward arcade to a full simulation incorporating physics models, telemetry, and bike setting.
