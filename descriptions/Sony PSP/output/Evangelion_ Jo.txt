Evangelion: Jo (ヱ ヴ ァ ン ゲ リ ヲ ン: 序, Evangerion: Jo) Is a video game based on Neon Genesis Evangelion. It was published by Bandai in 2009 for the PlayStation 2 platform, and a version for the PlayStation Portable was later released. The game completely simulates the events of the television series, similar to the game Evangelion 64. The player has full control of Evangelion Unit 01, in addition to interacting with the characters.
