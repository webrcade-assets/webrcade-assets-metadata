In the sequel to Brotherhood of the Blade, the story takes place eighteen years after your peaceful home city of Koryn Thal was invaded by a powerful and dangerous warlord. The warlord has taken over as emperor with a reign of oppression where the helpless people are either enslaved or killed by his vicious minions. During this time there is a secret race of shapeshifters who mysteriously begin to disappear, bringing up fear of a plot to eliminate them. As a result, they begin to flee towards the edge of the city towards the walls to evade whatever is capturing or killing their ancient race. You are a member of this shapeshifting race, and in the process of literally running for your life, you and several others come across a secret that brings hope to your people that peace and freedom may yet have a chance. At the beginning of the game you are allowed to choose a class, from Guardian, Mercenary, Disciple, Prowier and Scout. Each has unique characteristics and special abilities which presents an opportunity to play the game in vastly different natures. The character can be named and his or her look can be customized to your liking. In addition, you can assign points to the attributes of strength, dexterity, intelligence and stamina, each with a specific purpose and use. Fighting consists of special abilities, charged attacks and attacks of opportunity. Skill will play an important role in deciding whether or not you will be successful in any battle. A new aspect this game brings to Untold Legends is the multiplayer capability. You can play cooperative or team-based using wireless technology in a proximity or by playing online.
