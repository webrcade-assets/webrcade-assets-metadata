The year is 1985 and Flynn decides to hold a tournament in the digital world of TRON. Many programs come to participate and you are one of them (named Beta by default). You will participate in several events which get progressively harder, earn points and try to become the ultimate Grid Games champion. TRON: Evolution is a tie in with TRON: Legacy movie. Unlike TRON: Evolution for PC, X360 and PS3 it's basically a collection of mini-games which makes it very close to TRON: Evolution - Battle Grids for Wii. After the release of TRON: Evolution for PSP two mini-games from it were adapted for touch-screen controls and released as a separate game TRON: Legacy for iPad and iPhone.
