In a world covered almost entirely in water, there exist small islands that hide huge treasures. And where there's treasure, you can find treasure hunters called Diggers, who live for the thrill of the hunt. Barrel Casket was once one of the greatest Diggers, but he has now retired. He now cares for his young granddaughter, Roll, whose parents disappeared while on a dig. He had also discovered a strange baby boy in some ruins one time, who he also took under his care alongside Roll. He named the baby boy Rock Voulnut, and Rock grew up to be an expert Digger, sometimes known by his nickname, Rockman. Rockman Dash 2 - Episode 2: Great Inheritance (ロックマンDASH2 エピソード2 大いなる遺産 Rokkuman Dasshu Tsū - Episōdo Tsū: Ōinaru Isan) and know as Mega Man Legends 2 for the non-PSP North America release, is an action-adventure game released by Capcom in 2005 for the PSP, acting as the sequel to Mega Man Legends.
