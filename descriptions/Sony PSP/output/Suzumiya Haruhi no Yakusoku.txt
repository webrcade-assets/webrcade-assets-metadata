Suzumiya Haruhi no Yakusoku (The Promise of Haruhi Suzumiya) is an adventure visual novel developed and published by Bandai Namco Games. The games description is as follows: "As Noth High's Culture Festival draws near, all kinds of extraordinary incidents are happening once again! Can Kyon get the situation under control and see "The Adventures of Mikuru Asahina" to its completion?"
