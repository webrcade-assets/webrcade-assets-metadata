Space Invaders Pocket is a Shooter video game published by Taito Corporation released on September 12th, 2005 for the PSP. Includes: Space Invaders (1978) Monochrome version Cellophane version Upright version Colored version Space Invaders Part 2 (1979) Return of the Invaders (1985) Majestic 12: Space Invaders Part IV (1990) Space Invaders ’95 (1995)
