It's the Cinderella girl's time to shine! Megpoid has often been left in the shadows of the more well known Vocaloids such as Miku, Rin, and Len, but lately, her songs have been on top places in the Vocaloid charts. Perhaps the time for this other green haired girl has come. Come sway to her million-hit songs. The song list include her recent hits such as Megu Megu Fire Endless Night. Take high scores from the games to unlock new illustrations and costumes. You can decorate her room and go into the galleries inside. Famous illustrators such as Nou and Ryugu Tsukasa contribute artwork for the game, this is not a game any Vocaloid fan would want to miss.
