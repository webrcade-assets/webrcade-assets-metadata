WWII: Battle Over the Pacific is a World War II-themed arcade-style flight simulator. The player takes control of a pilot fighting for the Allies to dominate the Pacific. The action is viewed from a third-person perspective. As the free world's only hope, the protagonist fights enemy aircraft, tanks and battle ships, as well as bomb enemy installations. It is possible to land on carriers for rearming, repairing and even switching to another aircraft type.
