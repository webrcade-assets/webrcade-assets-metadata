The Secret Saturdays: Beasts of the 5th Sun is an action game based on the The Secret Saturdays animated series. According to the Aztecs earth has already exhausted four suns and that the current sun is the fifth and last one. This sun will end when the power of eight beasts from around the globe will fall in to one man’s hands. The evil Argost is after this power and it’s up to the player to stop him. Gameplay includes platforming with a grappling hook as well as sections where you have to beat-up enemies including bosses. The game offers ten levels in which you take control of all seven members of the Saturday family.
