Angus got abducted by aliens. While he was kept locked up, the aliens conquered Earth. Now with the help of a general and a female alien, it is your job to help Angus stop the alien invasion by all means possible. Angus Hates Aliens is a side-scrolling shooter where Angus has up to twelve upgradable weapons to choose from, but there are only nine inventory slots to assign them too. There are twelve levels to complete.
