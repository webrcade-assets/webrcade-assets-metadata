This is a re-release of two Playstation RPGs, which are loosely based on the classic Chinese novel Shuihuzhuan ("Water Margin"). In Suikoden, you play as the son of an imperial general, who joins a rebel organization and finds himself and his father fighting for the opposite camps. In Suikoden II, you are a young soldier who accidentally discovers that his captain is guilty of treason. Together with your best friend, you must find your place in a war between your homeland and a group of other countries. The graphics and the gameplay are the same as in the original release. Some extras were added: several additional scenes, the ability to view movie sequences and important events from the games, etc.
