Storm Lover takes you to one of the top private schools in Japan and drop you into a den of attractive boys. The game is not only about getting to know the boys and getting them to confess to you. It takes you past the honey moon period and to the stagnant period where you have to make important decisions and then back again. In this next 365 days in school, you have complete control over your love life. See which, or how many of the guys you will date. Be it sweet and innocent or spicy and turbulent, you get to live, and relive your ideal high school life in Storm Lovers.
