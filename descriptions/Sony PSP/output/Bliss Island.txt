In the game, players take on the role of Hoshi the Zwooph, a small blue fluffy creature with a long trunk, as he competes in loads of mini games that revolve around his unique ability to expel precision puffs of air at high velocity from his trunk - the game's signature and unique gameplay mechanic. Hoshi's great puffs can be used to bounce or control objects in the game world to complete individual challenges such as diverting pieces of falling fruit into hungry mouths, or blowing creatures around an obstacle course.
