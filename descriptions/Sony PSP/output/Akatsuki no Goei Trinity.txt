Akatsuki no Goei: Trinity for PlayStation 3 and PlayStation Portable is a non-adult trilogy port of PC (Windows) games from the same series. As an addition, this trilogy features new story elements, new graphics and CG events, new theme song and new opening movie. None of the games in the trilogy are available as standalone for either PSP or PS3 platforms. The titles included in this trilogy are:     Akatsuki no Goei      Akatsuki no Goei: Principal-tachi no Kyūjitsu      Akatsuki no Goei: Tsumibukaki Shūmatsuron
