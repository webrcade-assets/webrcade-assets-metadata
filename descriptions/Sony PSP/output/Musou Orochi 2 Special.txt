Bring your battlefield into the train or onto the bus. Musou Orochi 2 Special is now available on the Sony PSP. Game saves from the PlayStation3 version can be ported to the PSP so you have access to your handy warriors right from the start. Besides everything in the existing games, gamers also get to play with a set of original characters only accessible here. New to the Orochi universe is Rachel the demon hunter from Ninja Gaiden 2. This kind of adventure is right up her ally, loop her into your party and take her on a conquest. The new Battle Royal mode gives players a battle field to butt heads with fellow warriors they know in real life. Make use of the ad hoc network mode. These battles have special rules that make everything extra challenging.
