Ultraman Fighting Evolution 0 is a Fighting game from the series Ultraman Fighting Evolution. It is the fifth and currently last entry in the series. The game was released to coincide the release of the film, Ultraman Mebius & The Ultra Brothers. Both the movie and the game were created to commemorate the 40th anniversary of Ultraman. Story mode for the Ultra Brothers takes place some time before the movie and Ultraman Mebius' story mode takes place after Ultraman, Ultraseven, Ultraman Jack and Ultraman Ace got stuck on Earth after fighting U-Killersaurus.
