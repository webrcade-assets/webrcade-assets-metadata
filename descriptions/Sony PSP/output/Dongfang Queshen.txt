Using playing rules of China, Hong Kong and Taiwan, this authentic and first Chinese Mahjong game developed by Chinese game producers is now available on PSP. The eight characters all have  their own characteristics and mahjong-playing habits. With detailed hand movements, colourful screen effects and lively conversations by renowned voice actors, players feel as if they are playing a real game of mahjong. Apart from single-play, players can chose from multi-player online mode, simple tutorial mini-game that can train Mahjong skills and Challenge mode etc. Enjoy the fun of Magjong anytime and anywhere depending on your time and needs.
