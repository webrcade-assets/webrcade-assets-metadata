In New York City, a Koren Triad gang is terrorizing the city, attacking citizens in broad daylight and conducting bold robberies in front of hundreds of witness, as Kurt Wolf you lead an elite group of SWAT officers in order to stop the menace and discover a disturbing secret about the Triads, which could lead to the destruction of New York City. Each SWAT member is unique and has their own attributes and stats, which in upgradable after every level. The main goal of the game is to rescue hostages, take down terrorists and collect evidence. Players can earn extra points for each unharmed hostage and non-lethal takedowns.  The game also features a randomized AI placement, so each level replayed will have enemies and hostages and different locations than the last, for unlimited playing opportunities and players can create their own maps and play with or against other players using Ad-hoc.
