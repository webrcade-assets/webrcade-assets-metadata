Troops of otherworldly aliens are invading earth. To protect the planet and humanity, six chosen youths are recruited into the Scared Rider team under you, their commander. Gameplay is split into the adventure part and the battle part. Peer into the boys' everyday lives and get to know them better during their free time and send them to fight when the siren calls. The game comprises of 13 episodes, at the end of the day, will you be united with the man of your dreams or will you perish with the rest of the world?
