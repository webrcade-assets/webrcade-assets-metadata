Rurouni Kenshin: Meiji Kenkaku Romantan Kansei is a PSP 2D fighting game released on August 30, 2012. The game is a sequel to Rurouni Kenshin Meiji Kenkaku Romantan Saisen and has similar gameplay and graphics. The creators claimed to have improved the gameplay for this game by taking complaints about the first one to heart. Just like the first game the first print contained.
