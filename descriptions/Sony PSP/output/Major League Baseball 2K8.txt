The PSP version of MLB 2K8 is similar to its console cousins, featuring the same gameplay mechanics designed to fit the PSP, relying on the D-Pad to pick pitch locations and spots in the batter box as opposed to the analog for the 360, PS3 and PS2 versions. This version also has the game modes as well such as Season mode, Franchise mode, GM mode as well as the Home Run Derby. All the major teams are here as well as well all the teams as well as most of the major players and the ability to create a player. There is also online capability utilizing the Ad Hoc.
