Lucky Star: Ryōō Gakuen Ōtōsai (らき☆すた ～陵桜学園 桜藤祭～ ) is a visual novel game that was released in Japan on January 24, 2008 by Kadokawa Shoten for the PlayStation 2. A version for PSP was released on December 23, 2010. The game is a Romance-ADV Visual novel based on the Lucky Star characters. It is also the first type of Lucky Star in which the characters Kō Yasaka and Yamato Nagamori appeared. The game features the voice cast of the anime reprising their respective roles. A fan translation exists for this game and is currently a WIP.
