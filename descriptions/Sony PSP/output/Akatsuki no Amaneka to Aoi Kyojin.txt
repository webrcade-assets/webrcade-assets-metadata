Akatsuki no Amaneka to Aoi Kyojin is a Strategy game, developed by Kogado Studio and published by CyberFront.  In the game, the player creates a team, equips them with items and equipment and proceeds to explore the dungeons that lie underneath the capital. Any discovered treasures can either be handed over to the academy to gain fame, or sold on the black market to gain fortune. The game requires the player to maintain and balance the amount of fame and fortune, both of which are required in order to complete the game.
