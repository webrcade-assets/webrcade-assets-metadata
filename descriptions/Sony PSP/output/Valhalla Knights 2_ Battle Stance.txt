Valhalla Knights 2: Battle Stance is a PSN exclusive version of Valhalla Knights 2 which contains the following changes:     Additional warp points are included throughout the game.     Maximum party size is reduced from six to four.     More character customisation options.     19 new weapons     26 new pieces of armor and accessories.     7 new monsters.     19 new quest items.     4 new event characters.     10 new singleplayer quests.     10 new multiplayer quests.
