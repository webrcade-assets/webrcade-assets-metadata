Find a boy and shape him into an idol. Saotome Academy is a school for all aspiring idols and performance artist. You are Nanami Haruka, one of the talented composers who squeezed into the school. All these school idols have a one purpose, they are to sign with a production company and debut. Find the voice who could bring out the life in your songs. All the boys are potential stars, you have to draw out their full potential and their soft sides.
