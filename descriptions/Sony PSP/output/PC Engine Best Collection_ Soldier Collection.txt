This collection includes the four PC Engine (TurboGrafx-16) entries in Hudson Soft's Star Soldier series: •   Super Star Soldier (1990) •   Final Soldier (1991) •   Star Parodier (1992) •   Soldier Blade (1992) Also included are the "Special Version" contest versions of Final Soldier and Soldier Blade which were used at Hudson's Games Caravan events, as well as technique and hint videos narrated by Takahashi Meijin.
