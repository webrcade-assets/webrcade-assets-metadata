Toaru Majutsu no Index is an Action game, developed by Shade and published by Kadokawa, which was released in Japan in 2011. The story tells the tunes and maneuvers of the city Academia, a place where great advances are made in the field of science and where its students have superpowers. The protagonist is Touma Kamijou, a boy Level 0, level that is assigned to those unable to use any power. However, Touma has the Imagine Breaker and his right hand is able to override any special magic or ability. Despite this, Touma lives his day to day in a gentle way until he has to take care of Index, a nun girl who hides a great power.
