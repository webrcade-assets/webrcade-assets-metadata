Springtime of Life! Let’s enjoy our youth, full force! In spring they became seniors in high school…on account of their parents the twins returned to the rural town that they once lived. On the first day of school, they reunited with old friends. Soon after being reunited, after their long absence, their friends invited them to join a certain club. It is…… “Seishun-bu” There is only a year left before they graduate, and they established the club on a whim when they noticed that no club let them fully enjoy their youth. They decided to do everything "youth like" along with the other members of seishun-bu!…and it’s activities. The dear old town, a long-standing friend, meeting new friends. Creating memories of youth in their short time together. In a rural town surrounded by mountains and sea, now, a year to remember is about to begin. If you play as Chitose you can go after the male characters romantically (Ouji, Heita, Kanade, Tarou, Rihito, and Haiji) and if you play as Chihaya you can go after the female characters romantically (Arisu, Kotori, and Yoshino). There are also friendship routes for same sex characters and Yukihiko.
