Three PSP games, on one UMD, tailored for puzzle fans. Lemmings The Lemmings are on the loose and that can only mean one thing - trouble! Danger is barely a footstep away for the relentlessly marching hordes of cutesy, green-haired rodents. Go! Sudoku The crazily addictive phenomenon of Sudoku reaches new levels on PSP. Simple logic is the key to unlocking the number based puzzles. Go! Puzzle Easy to play but tricky to master, Go! Puzzle features three addictive games - Go! Puzzle Aquatica, Go! Puzzle Swizzle Blocks and Go! Puzzle Skyscraper.
