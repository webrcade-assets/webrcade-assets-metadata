Sands of Mars is a science fiction themed adventure game. You are Captain of the Starship Herman, and your mission is to pilot your spaceship to Mars, and recover the archaeologist Dr. Grady and other crew members, whom were lost in the Martian underground during the previous Explorer II mission. Your mission begins with outfitting your spaceship for the long voyage. You must ensure you have enough fuel, food, and oxygen for the long trip, as well as other supplies you'll need on the Red Planet. You must also choose your crew members, as each has special skills which are essential for survival on the harsh Martian climate.
