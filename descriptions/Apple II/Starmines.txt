Starmines is a 1-player space-themed arcade shooter for the Apple II. The player must guide their spacecraft through an endless minefield in space. The mines come out of nowhere, and the player must destroy them with their single laser cannon. The player can move quickly to the left and right, and can fight vertically to destroy mines as they approach. After destroying all mines within a quadrant, the player will jump into hyperspace to move to the next quadrant. There are a total of five levels. If the player is struck by a mine, they are destroyed.
