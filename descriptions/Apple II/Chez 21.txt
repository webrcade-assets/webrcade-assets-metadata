Chez 21 is a graphical 1 - 5 player blackjack game for the Apple II. The players compete against the dealer at a graphical blackjack table. Allows 2 different card designs. Each player starts with $2500, and players set their bet at the beginning of each hand against the dealer. Follows basic Las Vegas blackjack rules, with the goal being to draw cards to see who gets the closest to 21 without going over.
