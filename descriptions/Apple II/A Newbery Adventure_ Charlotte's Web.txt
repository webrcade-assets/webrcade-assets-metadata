A Newbery Adventure: Charlotte's Web is a multiple choice educational adventure game for young adults. Based upon the young adult novel Charlotte's Web, the player attempts to follow the story of Wilbur the Pig, as he attempts to avoid slaughter at the hands of Uncle Homer on his farm with help from Charlotte the spider and Templeton the rat. The player must correctly answer questions about the novel, answering 15 questions correctly over a period of 19 weeks, with each guess taking 1 week. If the player manages to answer the questions in time, Wilbur leaves a lasting impression at the county fair, and is saved from slaughter. Gameplay also includes a vocabulary test to help young readers learn several words from the novel.
