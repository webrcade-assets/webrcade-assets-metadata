Amazing Arithmetricks is a series of math learning games for the Apple II. The player can choose puzzle practice, or try to earn points by solving a set number of puzzles. Contains five separate puzzles, and the player can spend one of five coins to get hints. Each challenge has 3 difficulty levels. - Number Challenge: The player is given 4 numbers, and must devise an arithmetic expression to reach a defined answer. - Math-O-Grams: The player is given a math equation where numbers are represented by shapes or letters, and the player must determine what number they represent. - Magic Shapes: The player must complete shapes so that the sum of each row, column, and diagonal are equal - Twisted Tables: The player is given an addition or multiplication table, but must figure out which numbers fill in the x and y axis to make all the answers correct. - Nine Men's Morris: The player can play this classic board game if they complete all other puzzles.
