Math Blaster Mystery is a junior high school level math and problem solving game for multiple systems. There are 4 different math challenges, with each containing 4 levels of difficulty: -    Decipher the Code - The player must decipher a mathematical equation by trying to fill in blanks. Each turn, every correct number or operation is kept for the next round. The goal is to decipher the algorithm in the minimal number of turns. -    Follow the Steps - The player is walked through completing a word problem, by determining which information is important, how to solve the problem, and ultimately producing an answer -    Search for Clues - The player searches an old house by selecting the items and people pictured. Each selection will give a clue to the mystery number. When the player has gathered enough evidence, they can solve the problem, with the goal being to use as few clues as possible. -    Weigh the Evidence - A Tower of Hanoi based puzzle, where the goal is to place the desired weights on the scale to the far right.
