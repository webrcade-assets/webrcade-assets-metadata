The player controls a small spaceman via the keyboard or joystick, whose job is to capture alien creatures, and teleport them off the planet The player starts in a small maze, and alien creatures begin to appear within. The player can incapacitate an alien by shooting it with their laser, but once an alien is zapped, the player can't fire their gun any longer. The alien is wrapped up, and the player must drag it back to the awaiting teleporter, while avoiding other alien creatures. The teleporter will regularly change location, and the player can not fire again until the alien has been cleared. If all aliens are cleared, the player advances to the next difficulty level. The player has a total of 3 lives, and when all are lost, it's game over.
