Played from an overhead view, the player chooses which club to use, the direction of their shot, and the power they wish to use. Game play includes sand traps, water traps, fairways, and greens. Club choice, shot power, and wind affects shot distance, and if power is greater than 90%, accuracy is lost on a shot. The player manually chooses which direction they hit the ball, from 0 to 359 degrees. The player has 4 courses to choose from, with increasing tee to hole distance: Pitch & putt, Short Course, Regulation, and the Monster. When the player lands on the green, game play switches to overhead putting, where once again the player chooses direction and power of their putt.
