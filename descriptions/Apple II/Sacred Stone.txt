The sacred stone is a single player action game that can be run from keyboard or joystick. You are an adventuring archaeologist sent to find and appropriate the Sacred Stone. Your quest brings you to the entrance of an ancient underground shrine. Armed with a gun and 10 bullets, you must find the stone and escape with it.     Unfortunately, the stone's worshipers roam almost every passage. Be warned these mystical warriors are strong. Shooting them once will only wound them.     Legend has it that those followers who falter in their duties will be transformed into a pillar of stone. The shrine is full of supplies and various artifacts. You may even find some bullets or dynamite left be *cough* previous expeditions. Find the silver key to get into the holiest areas. Good luck!
