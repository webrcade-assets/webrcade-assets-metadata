Quations: The Crossmath Game is a 2 - 4 player math game for the Apple II. Similar to the board game Scrabble, except players take turns placing numbers on a board to make mathematical equations. Players score points based upon the number and point value of the tiles placed. Equations can be placed horizontally and vertically, and there are bonus squares present on the board. Each turn, the player receives new tiles for the ones placed, or can use a turn to request new tiles. Players can choose addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, or a combination of these problems. Allows gameplay for 1 - 4 players with the addition of computer opponents.
