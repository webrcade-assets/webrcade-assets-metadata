Paranoiak is a humorous French text adventure set inside a city. The game screen consists of a graphics area near the top, and a text area with a prompt near the bottom. The player's character is plagued by a large number of conditions, including an Oedipus Complex, asthma, amnesia, dizziness, shyness, and yet needs to overcome these while walking around the city and avoid getting taken in at the psychiatric hospital. At the same time he is completely out of money so most locations are completely inaccessible to him.
