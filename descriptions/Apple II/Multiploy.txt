Multiploy is an educational game for the Apple II. The player controls a small turret in the bottom center of the screen, as mathematical equations rain from above. The player must quickly type in the answers to the questions, and if they type a correct answer, their turret blasts the equation. If the player is too slow, the pieces get close to the turret and fire back, destroying the player. The player receives a score based upon how well they answer questions. The player can choose from 3 difficulty levels, and can perform addition, subtraction, multiplications, and division questions.
