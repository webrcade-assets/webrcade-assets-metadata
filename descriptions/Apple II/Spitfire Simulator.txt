The time is early 1941, and the place is England. The Battle of Britain is underway. German airplanes are invading the skies of England, and it is your mission to help rid the skies of their presence. The player controls a Spitfire fighter from a 1st person perspective. It features takeoff and landing, pursuing and attacking 3-D aircraft, and scoring for hitting targets and completing missions. Played from a 1st person perspective using wire frame graphics.
