Game of the States is a collection of 5 games for one or two players. In Name the State you must correctly identify the name of the state shown on the screen. The Capital City module will have you naming the capital for the state shown on the screen. The Abbreviation Game teaches state abbreviations by having you identify the state based upon the abbreviation or doing the opposite and typing in the abbreviation for the state. In Name the Neighbors you must name all of the states which neighbor the state shown on the screen. Finally, in Major Cities you must select the city which is not a major city in the state shown.
