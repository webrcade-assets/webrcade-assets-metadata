Pandemonium is a 1-player spelling game. Players are presented with a 5 x 5 grid, where they are randomly given letters to fill in, with more common letters and an appropriate mix of vowels and consonants. Gameplay can be timed or unlimited, with the goal being to create as many words as possible horizontally, vertically, and diagonally. The game has a built in dictionary, and the player is automatically scored at the end of each game. Bonus squares are present on the board which increase the player's score when used, and the player scores more points for longer words. Gameplay options allow for mobile, which allow for some pieces to be moved at the end of the game, and bypass, which allows some unused letters to be passed.
