Simply use any methods you like (such as Napoleonic miniatures) and enter the off· line combat results into the computer. It will incorporate them into the strategic game. Napoleon's Campaigns: 1813 & 1815 is a strategy game simulating Napoleon's Leipzig and Waterloo campaigns. There are two scenarios to choose from. First there is Waterloo, June 1815. In this scenario you need to destroy the Prussian army or Anglo-Allied army before they can be united. The second one is Leipzig, October 1813. In this scenario Napoleon has to utilize his central position to destroy the Allied army, or failing this, to open a supply line back to France before being surrounded and crushed.
