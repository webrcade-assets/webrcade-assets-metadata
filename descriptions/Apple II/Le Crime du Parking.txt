Le crime du parking (The Crime of the Parking Lot) is a French text adventure where players need to investigate the death of the young woman Odile Conchoux. She is found in the parking lot of the supermarket where she worked, naked and inside a trolley. The game screen consists of a graphics area near the top, and a text area with a prompt near the bottom. The player needs to search the environment for clues and talk to the different characters to discover a lead. The game manual include a map of the different locations to make navigation easier, as well as a list of all the characters.
