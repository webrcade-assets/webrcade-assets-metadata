Renju is a 1 - player computerized version of the Japanese board game for the Apple II. Played on a 15 x 15 board, the player can either be black or white, and each player takes turns placing a corresponding colored stone on the board. All gameplay occurs against a computer opponent. The goal is to place 5 stones in a row before the computer opponent can do the same. Renju has numerous rules which govern opening moves in the game.
