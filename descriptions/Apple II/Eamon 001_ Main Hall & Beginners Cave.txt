Donald Brown's 1980 game actually was the first installment in a long series of adventures. It spawned an entire movement of games using the Eamon system and is thus of obvious historical importance. This adventure is rather sparse as it is intended as a trying ground for new players.     The Beginner's Cave has been set up by the warlord as a service to all Free Adventurers, giving them a chance to try their skills in a not-too-dangerous setting. Let us all toast the warlord for restocking the cave daily! This adventure is part of the Eamon universe. Like all Eamon adventures, it is an RPG-style game, mostly using a two-word parser. Because Eamon is a modular system, you may bring a character and equipment from other adventures into this one. This game was released on the original master disks and serves both as an introduction to the game for new players and as an easy means for beginner characters to obtain some modest treasure and experience.
