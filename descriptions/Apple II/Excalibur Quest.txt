At a time when the earth was still populated by mythological monsters, man had to use pure force to fight.  Excalibur Quest retraces the trials that the knight knight had to overcome to receive the initiatory sword Excalibur.  Strength, astuteness, composure, reflection are the qualities he had to demonstrate. This game is a French language, single player text adventure with graphics from the team of     Jean-Claude Feraudet, Jean-Jacques Perrody, and Philippe Fertin released by Excalibur in 1986.
