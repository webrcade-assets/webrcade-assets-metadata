The Human Fly is an arcade game for the Apple II. The player controls a superhero by named The Human Fly, whose goal is to climb to the top of a skyscraper. The player must steadily ascend, and can move up, left, or right. A number of hazards hamper the climb, including: angry gorillas, falling flower pots, angry policemen, unpredictable crows, and window washers. If the player is struck by any of these, they fall to their doom on the streets below. A passing balloon can be latched onto, to give the player a lift up. A small display on the left of the screen shows the player's progress.
