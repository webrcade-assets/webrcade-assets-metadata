A Homestarrunner.com game come to life on the incredibly up-to-date Apple II! Based somewhat on the King's Quest series this demake from Flash game to Apple began in 2021 and continues to progress. You play as the character Rather Dashing, a humble peasant who's thatched roof cottage has been fully burninated by a Trogdor dragon. With nothing left to lose and a whole afternoon to kill you swear to get revenge on Trogdor in the name of burninated peasants everywhere.
