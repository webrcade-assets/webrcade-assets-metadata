Super Puckman is a 1-player Pac-Man clone for the Apple II. The player moves about a maze, trying to pick up all the pellets. They can be Pack-Man or Pack-Gril. The player is chased by 4 ghosts, who become temporarily vulnerable if the player picks up one of the power pellets in the corners of the maze. The player starts with 3 lives. There are 9 levels of difficulty, and the player can control game speed and color.
