The official computer software version of MTV's ridiculous and entertaining trivia game show. Now you can play the gameshow that started in the basement right on your PC! Just answer the silly questions about Rock'n'Roll and junk trivia and win lots of points! Senseless? You bet!! Fun? Always! You'll be the envy of your friends, and best of all you don't have to be smart to win. Over 1,000 challenging questions in 9 absurd, thematic, tasteless and humorous categories. Crazy interruptions such as "Off the Air", "Home Shopping Zone", and "Ranger Bob", just like the real show. End game round where only the best win it all. Single or group play options.
