The player takes control of the U.S.S. Enterprise, as it defends the 64 quadrant Confederation from enemy Machiavellians during a limited time frame. The player must control the ship's limited energy while maintaining the health of their ship's systems. The player's ship is equipped with torpedoes and positron lasers. The player must warp to different sectors in search of enemy vessels, and can resupply at starbases. The player is also able to beam down to individual planets to search for rare trilithium crystals which can improve the ship's energy usage.
