In Search of Karine, in English. The illustrious city of GRISYMAZE is in the hands of the tyrant ZEBUS. He holds Princess Karine prisoner, in a hidden place in the city. Your mission is to deliver the Princess, after defeating Zebus and all his troops. At the start of the adventure, you must create the 4 characters who will constitute your team. During your adventure, the graphic window, right of your notch, shows you the road you are going The city of Grisymaze has around fifteen different Buildings that you must, of course, visit. The most fearsome monsters hide in the Dungeons below the castle.
