In Microzine issue #34, "The Case of the Missing Rose" is a mystery adventure which has the player assuming the role of Ed Minkly searching for the whereabouts of the white rose.  "Secret Sentences" is a word guessing game similar to hang-man.  "The Mystery of the Olympic Impostors" asks the player to investigate the disappearance of 3 olympic athletes with a 1 hour time limit.  Finally, "FYI: A Database of Inventions" is a database of over 100 inventions.
