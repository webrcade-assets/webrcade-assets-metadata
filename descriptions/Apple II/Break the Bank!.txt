Break the Bank! is a blackjack teaching tool and simulator. Designed to teach players techniques for improving their skill at blackjack, the program offers tutorials in four levels of blackjack play, from best play without card counting to advanced card counting techniques. The program contains drills and flashcards designed to improve the player's blackjack play. The player can also input their own strategies, and see how they compete against the strategies in game. Finally, there is an actual graphical animated blackjack game, where the player competes against the computer to put their blackjack skills into practice. Game rules follow typical Las Vegas blackjack play.
