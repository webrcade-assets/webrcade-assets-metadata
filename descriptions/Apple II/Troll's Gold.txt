Troll's Gold is a strategy game. Gold had been hidden in the bottom right corner of a 6 x 6 grid. The player enters from the top left, and must gather the gold and return to the exit without getting caught by the troll. Each turn, exits randomly open and close on the boxes that separate different parts of the dungeon. The player must move between boxes when the doors are open, while avoiding the troll, who always moves towards the player.
