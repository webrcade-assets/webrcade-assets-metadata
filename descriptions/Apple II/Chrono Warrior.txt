In Chrono Warrior you travel through different eras of time and with each age to travel to features a different style of gameplay. -    Stone age: collect all the pieces of your time portal and go through it. Beware of the dinosaurs. -    Roman empire: you ride a chariot in a race and must collect 5 gold coins to proceed. -    Medieval time: you are Robin Hood and must go through a maze to rob from the rich and give to the poor. Do this three times to proceed. -     Civil war: you ride a balloon and must collect five energy ribbons to proceed. You must avoid the cannon fire as you do so. -    1985: avoid cars and gather mail playing as a postman.
