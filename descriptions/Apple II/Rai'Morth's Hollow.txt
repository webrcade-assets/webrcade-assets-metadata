Rai'Morth's Hollow is a computerized fantasy role playing game. The game is designed to assist game masters develop and role play a computerized scenario with other players. The player starts in the town of Three Rivers, and describes in details the local inn and available locations the gathered party can travel. Each location the players can travel to provide detailed description, as well as possible role playing opportunities for the game master to employ. Gameplay can also be single-player as the game master explores the multitude of opportunities by themselves. All gameplay options are menu based, and at any time the game master can generate a fiend for the players to battle. The software also generates appropriate loot from battles or searching the area. In each scenario, the game provides possible role playing opportunities for the gamemaster. Menu options also allow movement to different locations.
