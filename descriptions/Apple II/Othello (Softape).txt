Othello is a computer adaptation of the board game. It's a one player game played against an AI opponent. It's played on an 8x8 board where the keyboard is used to move a cursor between playable squares. The player can choose to start or let the opponent start. Like in the original board game the two players take turns adding stones to the board in attempt to trap the opponent's stones which then will change colour. When the board is full the game ends and the player with the most amount of stones wins.
