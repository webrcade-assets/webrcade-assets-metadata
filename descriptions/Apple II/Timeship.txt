The date is August 25th, 600,000,000 A.D. and you stand beneath a blazing, unmoving sun. No crime has been committed on Earth for more than three hundred million years, until now. As a member of the Time Travellers' Guild your mission is to solve the mysterious crime using the bizarre clues and peculiar witnesses you encounter. Designed as a role-playing adventure system, the player starts in the Time Travellers' Guild Hall. There, the player sets their statistics, including: strength, endurance, intelligence, speed and others. The player also needs to set abilities with a variety of weapons. The player can purchase clothes, equipment, weapons and armor. The player visits a number of graphical locations during their adventure.
