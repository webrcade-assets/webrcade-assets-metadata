The Breckenridge Caper of 1798 is a text adventure game for the Apple II. The year is 1798, and given Napoleon Bonaparte's recent victory over British forces at Giza, Egypt, the British government has made every effort possible to obtain knowledge of Napoleon's future plans. Acting in capacity of Ezekiel Breckenridge, an intelligence officer for the British High Command, you have been given explicit orders to patrol a city block in the southern section of Portsmouth. Your mission is to obtain secret documents from a diplomat who's arriving from Calais, France.
