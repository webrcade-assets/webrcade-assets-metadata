Germ Lab is a science fiction themed text adventure for the Apple II. All commands are via text VERB NOUN input. The player takes on the role of a secret agent, whose goal is to break into the North American Biological Initiative Laboratory and render the apparently insane Dr. Crother Turcot harmless before he can release into the atmosphere Procrox-s, the virulent, self-mutating anthrax/swine flu derivative that could kill 99.98% of the world's population. The player must outsmart a number the facility's defenses including computer defense systems, guards, and dogs, as they work their way down through a number of the facility's levels. The player must ultimately confront Dr. Turcot, and prevent the release of the deadly infection.
