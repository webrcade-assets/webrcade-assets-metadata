You have been dropped off in the jungle wilderness by a helicopter, armed only with a supply of food. The player must attempt to survive a set number of days, while trying to find the four diamonds which are lost in the jungle. During this search, the player must contend with environmental hazards including rainstorms, floods, thirst, and heat exhaustion. The player must sleep during the nights, or they will be too weak to continue, and must also discover appropriate shelter. Each turn typically takes one hour, and time is displayed at the top of the screen The game has a difficulty level from 1 - 9, which determines how frequent and dangerous the environmental dangers are. The player has a total of 12 - 18 days to find all the treasures, before the helicopter returns to pick up the player again. All commands are via VERB NOUN commands, and the player has a small available inventory. There is a save and restore feature available.
