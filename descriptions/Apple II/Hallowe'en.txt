The player controls a magician that must collect a number of green pumpkins from a series of platforms. The character can move left and right, and can jump over enemies. They are also able to shoot a magical bolt which can erode a hole in the platform's floor blocking enemies. The player must climb up a number of ladders, and must avoid enemies including: spiders, choppers, bolts, skulls, bolts, and duplicators. If the player is struck by an enemy they lose a life. After collecting all pumpkins on a level, the player advances to a more difficult stage. The player starts with 4 lives, and when all are lost it's game over.
