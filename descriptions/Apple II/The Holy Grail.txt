The Holy Grail is a text adventure in which the player controls a young prince in search of the famous titular object. The Emperor has promised him the rule over the country should he succeed. However, his malevolent half-brother Balnard intends to use the Holy Grail for his own shady purposes. The interaction with the game world is performed by examining locations and inputting text commands. There are also battle sequences and other instances where the player is urged to react quickly.
