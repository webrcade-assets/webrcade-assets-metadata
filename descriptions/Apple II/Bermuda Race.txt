Bermuda Race is a challenging simulation of the blue-water sailboat race from Newport, Rhode Island to Bermuda, some 635 miles across the open Atlantic. You're racing against those who set the actual Bermuda Race record and also against the top previous simulation players. To sail your best, keep a steady hand in control of your heading, sails, and centerboard, but watch out for the weather and the unexpected! By effective use of the highly precise information supplied by the computer, you may win a place in the Player's Hall Of Fame! For 1 or 2 players, beginner to expert, Bermuda Race is not only fun, it's educational. Interactive tutorials help you learn everything you need to know about sailing with this simulation. You can chart your progress on high-quality video screens that show each leg of the race.
