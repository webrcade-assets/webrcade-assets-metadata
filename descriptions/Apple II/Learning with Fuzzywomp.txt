There are four kinds of different educational games intended for kids. They include knowledge and numerical order (the digits, which are represented as bubbles, fly out from a pipe, and you must help FuzzyWOMP blow them out in the proper order); digit definition (the clown shows the digit and FuzzyWOMP must dispense the right amount of balls - if the number is correct, the clown juggles them. If it's wrong, he gets upset); calculating, comparing, and memorizing (a domino is shown and is then hidden, and FuzzyWOMP has to choose the same one from the six possible variants); and shooting (after creating your assistant you have to shoot FuzzyWOMP).
