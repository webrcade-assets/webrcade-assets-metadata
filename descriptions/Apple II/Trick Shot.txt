Trick Shot is a 1 - 4 player pool simulation for the Apple II. Trick Shot has a top-down graphical display of a pool table, and attempts to accurately simulate the physics of pool. Uses the same engine as earlier released Pool 1.5. Contains 4 types of gameplay: snooker, billiard, open table, and 3-ball. New features include Trick Shot mode, where the player defines the location of the balls on the table, allowing them to perform custom trick shots. The player uses paddles to move their aim 360 degrees. The player can control the strength of their shot from 1 - 8, and 9 locations where on the white ball to hit, to control ball spin. Players can define board friction, and the speed of gameplay. Players can also review their past shot with a replay feature.
