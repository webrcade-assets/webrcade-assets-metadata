Super Othello is a one-player adaptation of the board game for the Apple II. One player game versus a single difficulty computer opponent. The game is played on an 8x8 board where the keyboard is used to move a cursor between playable squares. The player can choose to start or let the opponent start. Like in the original board game the two players take turns adding stones to the board in an attempt to trap the opponent's stones which then will change color. When the board is full the game ends and the player with the most amount of stones wins.
