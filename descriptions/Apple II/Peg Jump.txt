Peg Jump is a peg solitaire game played on a triangular board with fifteen holes. The number of holes that are occupied by pegs can be decided by the player before starting out. The aim of the game is to remove pegs from the board by moving neighboring pegs over them. The key is played with keyboards and moves are made by entering the numbers of the holes a peg should be moved between. Once the game has been completed, it is possible to watch the computer solving the same board.
