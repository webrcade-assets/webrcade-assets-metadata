M.I.R.V. is a military themed action game for the Apple II. A MIRV is a Multiple Independently targetable Reentry Vehicle, a missile capable of striking multiple targets. The player controls a small United States Air Force jeep, whose job is to collect these missiles as they fall to the ground. If the player misses a missile, they explode, and a life is lost. The player gains 10 points for each missile collected. The player has a total of 3 lives, and when all are lost, it's game over.
