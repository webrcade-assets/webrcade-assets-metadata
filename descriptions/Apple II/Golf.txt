Golf is a 1 - 2 player graphical golf simulation for multiple systems. Played from an overhead view, the player must battle through 18 randomly generated golf courses. Each course contains a water trap and a green. The player selects the direction they wish to hit the ball, and which club they want to use, from 0 - 9. The player loses a swing if their ball goes out of bounds or lands in the water. If the player successfully gets to the green, they enter a putting phase where they merely have to select the direction to putt. The game keeps track of the player's score through 18 holes.
