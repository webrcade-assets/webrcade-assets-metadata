Wall Street is a stock market simulation game. The goal is simply to earn as much money as possible by buying stocks cheap and selling them high. Each day, the player views the stock quotes and financial reports of the companies and tries to make educated choices based on that information. When starting a new game, one of five levels of infoservice can be chosen - a higher level costs more per week but improves the quality of the provided information. This game uses real company names and the stock quotes can be printed.
