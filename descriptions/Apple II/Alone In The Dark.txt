-A suspicious suicide. A chilling curse. A malevolent power. And a wicked dark secret. This is Derceto, legendary Louisiana mansion—home to the late Jeremy Hartwood and a host of unspeakable evils. -Your quest for truth drives you to investigate the old house. But you didn't know you'd be thrown into the daily nightmare that was Jeremy Hartwood's life. You're not prepared for the tortuous howls that resound from deep within the halls. What waits for you down the endless, shadowy corridors, in the gloom-filled rooms? What is the source of that heavy sense of evil that fills the very house itself? -The specter of personal danger is real and MacPlay's 3D polygon graphics make it all the more frightening. You'll encounter hideous, hidden monsters that come at you from every corner. View each room in a dramatic cinematic sweep of sinister detail from a variety of different camera angles. From the creaking stairs to the dank catacombs, you are plunged into the eerie depths of Derceto. -A It is too late to turn back. You must go on and face it alone..and in the dark.
