Pick-A-Dilly Pair is a 1 - 6 player tile matching game for the Apple II. Players are presented with a series of 36 tiles, which each contain an animated graphic when they are selected. Players take turns selecting tiles to try to match them. When tiles are successfully matched, they are removed from the board, and the player who obtains the most matches wins. There are several variants to increase replay value. Flash shows the underlying graphic for a brief time period, making it more difficult to remember positions. There options to match 3 or 4 tiles at a time, and one option includes jokers and villains, which will act as bonus cards or cause a player to lose their turn.
