Captain Cuckoo is a graphical adventure game for the Apple II. The player controls the eponymous Captain Cuckoo, as he attempts to become a secret agent. Gameplay is via text commands via VERB NOUN entries, and the scenes the character is in are graphically displayed. In game, the player must find something to eat at the bar. They then must discovery some money on the side of the road to buy a special elixir, which is drank to get a special letter from a spy. This letter can be given to the chief spy whom makes you a super hero.
