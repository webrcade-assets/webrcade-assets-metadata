As the duly-designated wizard assigned to the kingdom of Vorr, you have been chosen by the king to save the Sparkstone from the forces of evil. Without it, the kingdom will be left defenseless (and without a lemony-fresh scent). Gameplay is designed as "choose your own adventure", where preceding actions during the story have direct repercussions on how the story ultimately plays out. Gameplay choices are entirely menu-based, where the player chooses the desired action from a set of story branches.
