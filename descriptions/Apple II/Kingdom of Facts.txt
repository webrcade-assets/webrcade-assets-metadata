Kingdom of Facts is a 1 - 4 player trivia game based upon the characters of Olin in Emerald. Each player chooses their name, and their iconic representation as 1 of 4 characters: Olin, Vargor, Elf, or Anara. Players can choose the difficulty level of their questions, the number of points required for victory, and the number of seconds available to answer each question. Each player takes a turn picking one of four categories at random, and then are presented with a multiple choice question with four answers. The player receives one point for each correct answer. Players can make their own custom questions to extend gameplay.
