Roulette is a computer adaptation of the casino game for one or two players. The screen shows a top down view of the roulette table and the game is played with the keyboard or a light pen. First the player chooses how many numbers the bet will cover, then which spot it goes on and finally the amount of the bet is entered. All standard roulette bets are allowed. Once bets have been made an animated wheel spins and shows the winning number.
