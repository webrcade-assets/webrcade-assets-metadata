The Elysian Fields and Other Greek Myths (Elysian Fields) is an educational graphic adventure game about Greek mythology, where the player must find a way back to the present.  The game is the work of Mark Novembrino: "The Elysian Fields was released by American Eagle Software in 1984. I began development on the program about a year and a half earlier when I was 13 years old. Of course, at the time, I was certainly not the only teenager developing software, and my young age was not unique in this field.
