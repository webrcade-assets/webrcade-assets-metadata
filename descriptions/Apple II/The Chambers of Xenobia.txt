It is the 15th century, and you are an adventurer who has come to seek his fortune in The Chambers of Xenobia, a dungeon that used to be populated by an evil warlock. Dangerous evil creatures roam the halls, and you must defeat them to claim the warlock's bountiful treasures. The Chambers of Xenobia is a text-based adventure where instructions are entered via VERB NOUN commands. The goal is to navigate the dungeon and recover the 13 treasures that are within, and return them to the entryway. The player must battle with the denizens of the dungeon, which involves turns of the player and monster attacking. The probability of hitting a monster is random, but if they are struck, they are destroyed. The player can take a total of 3 hits during an adventure, or they lose the game.
