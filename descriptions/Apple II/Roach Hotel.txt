Roach Hotel is an arcade game for the Apple II The player controls a large foot, that can move left to right, and slam down on roaches as they attempt to invade the Roach Hotel. Roaches invade from the left and right, trying to take cover within the hotel. The goal is to stop roaches by stamping them before they can reach the hotel. Large spiders will stream from the bottom of the screen, and can damage the foot if hit from the side. The hotel will slowly fill with roaches, as they infest multiple floors. Players are awarded points based upon the number of bugs crushed, with the goal to maximize score. The player has a total of 3 feet, and it's game over when the hotel is full, or all feet are lost.
