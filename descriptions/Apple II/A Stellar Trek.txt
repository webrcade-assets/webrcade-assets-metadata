A Stellar Trek is an early Star Trek variant for the Apple II. The player takes control of the Enterprise, as they try to destroy all Klingon vessels located in a 64 quadrant galaxy that is further divided into 10 x 10 sectors. The galaxy included Federations and Romulan starbases, stars, planets, and black holes. The player controls all ship functions, including: movement via warp and impulse engines, use of starship weaponry, maintenance of energy reserves, and repair of incurred damage. The player has access to short ranges scanners which investigate the nearby sectors and long range scanners that provide limited information on surrounding quadrants. Local planets can be mined for dilithium to increase energy. There are 5 levels of difficulty and 3 game lengths available.
