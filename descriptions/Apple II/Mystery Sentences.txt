Mystery Sentences is a word game for one to four players. The players choose from a multitude of topics, and receives a sentence where most of the letters have been blanked out. Each player starts with 250 points, and the player gains points by correctly guessing individual words, or guessing the entire sentence. The player loses points by asking for clues, revealing letters, or incorrectly guessing words or sentences. The sooner a sentence is solved, the more points a player receives. The player with the most points at the end of several rounds is the victor.
