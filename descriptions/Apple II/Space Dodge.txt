Space Dodge is a 1-player space-themed arcade game for the Apple II. The player controls a spaceship which can move horizontally and fire vertically. The player confronts a number of spaceships as asteroids stream towards the player. Alien vessels fire towards the player, and the player must dodge this. When the player destroys all enemy vessels, they progress to a more difficult challenge. The player has 3 ships, and when all are lost it's game over.
