Trusty bow in hand, you make your way across the ice slick battlefield. Before you loom the ice pits, a grotesque collection of hundreds of creatures. Below this nightmarish field lies the High Master waiting for you in his icy kingdom. The player controls a smiley face named Tom or a smiley face with sunglasses named Ray on a large open field. The player can move in 8 directions, and can also fire an arrow in these 8 directions. The player combats a number of monsters, including: slimes, plebes, willowisps, and widowmakers. Gameplay allows for 2-player cooperative or competitive play, as well as single player options. Players can purchase special attacks and pick up bonus arrows during gameplay. The player must destroy all monsters as they appear on the screen, until eventually they reach the High Master.
