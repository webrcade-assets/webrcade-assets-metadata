Space Raiders (not to be confused with the Star Raiders clone from 1981) is a 1983 single-player space-themed arcade game for the Apple II. The player controls a spaceship which can move horizontally and fire vertically. The player confronts a number of different sized alien UFOs, which fire towards the player. When the player destroys all enemy vessels, they progress to an exactly identical level once again. The player has 3 ships, and when all are lost it's game over.
