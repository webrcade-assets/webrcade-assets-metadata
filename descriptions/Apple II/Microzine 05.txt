"Microzine" is an educational disk-based magazine published by Scholastic between 1983 and 1992.  Issue number 5 includes "Pirates of the Soft Seas," a Twistaplot Adventure that has the player pit wits against a band of pirates. The issue also includes "Story Teller" a simple word/picture processor designed to help kids write and illustrate simple stories "Amazing Robot II" is essentially a small, less complicated version of LOGO Finally, "Fences" is an electronic version of the paper and pencil game "Dots"
