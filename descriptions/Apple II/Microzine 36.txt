Issue # 36 of Scholastic's Microzine series has another 4 programs for grade school children.  The first title, "The Case of the Crafty Caper," is a mystery adventure.  The second program, "Trap Doors," is a puzzle game that has the player avoiding trap doors in a spooky old house.  The third title, "Dr Think and the Idea Factory," has the player explore the thoughts of past inventors.  The final title, "It Fits!," is a puzzle game.
