Bloody Murder is a 2-player knife fighting game for the Apple II. Played from a pseudo-3D perspective, two players try to murder the other by throwing knives across the screen. Each player can move horizontally, one in the foreground, and one in the background, and can throw a knife careening towards the other player. If a player is struck by a knife, they are injured or killed. The game is over, when one player loses a life.
