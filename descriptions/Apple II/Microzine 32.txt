Issue number 32 was the beginning of the end of Scholastic's long running successful edutainment series, Microzine.  This was the first issue to forgo the color software packaging with a generic cover.  That being said the software quality was just as high as ever.  This issue includes "Eyes on the World", "FYI: A Database", "A Night at Fleener's", and "The Case of the Unidentified Flying Alien."
