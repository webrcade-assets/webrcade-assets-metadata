Cross City is a 1-player driving arcade game for the Apple II. The player drives a car which travels horizontally left and right, but can also jump. Portrayed from a side view, the player can jump between platforms and jump over other cars. The player picks up red tanks, which can be thrown at other cars to destroy them. The player also picks up blue tanks, and when ten tanks are collected, the player advances to the next level. If the player strikes another car, their vehicle is destroyed. The player starts with 3 cars, and when all are destroyed, it's game over.
