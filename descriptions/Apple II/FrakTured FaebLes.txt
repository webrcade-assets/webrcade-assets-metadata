FrakTured FaebLes is a short nursery rhyme themed graphic adventure game. The player enters commands via text VERB NOUN commands, and most puzzles are inventory based. The player must visit a number of nursery rhyme characters to solve their problems and gather items. The player has a limited carrying capacity. Numerous dead end situations can be found by using items in the wrong order. An evil witch has cast a spell upon the player, and they have been deposited into the middle of a strange forest. During their adventure, the player encounters Rip van Winkle, a pig playing golf, Goldilocks and the Three Bears, the Queen of Hearts, a prince, and other characters. Ultimately, you must find Sleeping Beauty who, if you find a breath mint, will ride off into the sunset with you after a kiss.
