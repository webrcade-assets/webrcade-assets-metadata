Fight the approaching hordes of Invader ships. Use your powerful laser cannons before the enemy gets you. A Space Invaders clone, where the player must move left and right to avoid shots fired by oncoming enemy creatures. There are objects to hide behind to stop the enemy's shots from hitting you. Each creature is worth 10, 20, or 30 points. Occasionally, space ships dart across the screen, and they are worth between 50 to 300 points. When a spaceship reflects a bullet, if the player hits the ship, again they are awarded 500 points. The player earns an extra life for every 1000 points. The game ends when the player has run out of lives.
