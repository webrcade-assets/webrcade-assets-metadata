E.T. Comes Back is a brief unlicensed graphical adventure based upon the movie E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial. The player plays as 10-year old Elliott, the young boy who had just helped E.T. return to his home. The player enters commands via VERB NOUN text commands, and can explore his home and the area immediately outside of the house. The player finds E.T. once again outside of his house in the shed, and can lure E.T. out with a geranium. The player briefly meets their brother and sister, whom appear to be characters from the comic Peanuts. The player then borrows their parents' car, and drives E.T. out to the rendezvous point with E.T.'s alien companions
