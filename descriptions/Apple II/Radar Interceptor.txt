Radar Interceptor is a simulator of a radar scope. The player is being attacked by an incoming airborne enemy vehicle and the task is to bring it down with a missile. The enemy is represented on the radar by blips that appear at a certain frequency. The player's missile which is controlled with a game paddle is also represented by blips (although slightly smaller ones). The player's task is simply to move the missile so that it intercepts the enemy vehicle. When that happens the player earns one point and another enemy attack is launched. If the player fails to make an interception the enemy will penetrate the defences and gain a point.
