Shuttle Intercept is a space-themed arcade game for the Apple II. The player controls a space shuttle which can move vertically up and down. Satellites, asteroids, UFOs, and Rockets stream from the right of the screen. The shuttle is armed with a laser that fires to the right, and can shoot down oncoming dangers. The shuttle has the ability to catch satellites with the mechanical arms that protrude from the top of the vehicle. After several rounds of incoming objects, the player enters a meteor storm with a number of fast moving asteroids to avoid, and then returns to regular speed. The player has a total of 3 shuttles, when all are lost, it's game over.
