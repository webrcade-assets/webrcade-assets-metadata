The player controls a rocket ship sitting on a launch pad in space. The goal is to launch the rocket, and land it on the second landing pad which is present on the screen. To control this, the player must supply vertical and horizontal thrust. Thrust is limited by your ship's fuel supply, which ranges from 250 to 750. If the rocket strikes the mountain separating the two landing pads, or crashes too hard into the landing pad, it is destroyed. The player received a final score based upon the amount of fuel used.
