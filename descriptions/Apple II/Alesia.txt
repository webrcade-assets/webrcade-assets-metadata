A French language text adventure. You are in 52 BC in a small Gallic village near Alesia surrounded by Roman legions. Your supplies being almost exhausted, and the attack imminent, the chief of Tartopum (pronounced 'Tartopom' - Applepie!) charges you with the following mission: You must infiltrate the enemy camp and steal the battle plan of general Micrus Ordinatus (- Microcomputer) so that the Gauls win the battle. The mission is very perilous and you will have to use your intuition and finesse to carry it out, because the traps are numerous... Maybe you will manage to change the course of history...
