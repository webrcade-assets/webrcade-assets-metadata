Star Dance is a space-themed arcade game for the Apple II. Gameplay is divided into a 3 x 3 grid, displayed on the top and bottom of the screen. Within this grid, bounce stars, up to 3 at a time. The player uses a cluster of 9 keyboard commands or the joystick to position the cursor under one of the bouncing stars. The star can then be destroyed by allowing it to bounce on the cursor, or by shooting it with a nova. The player's goal is to maximize the number of stars destroyed during the time allotted.
