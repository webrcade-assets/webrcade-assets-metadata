Zoo Builder is an educational game for the Apple II. The player designs a zoo, and can choose from either a hot or cool climate to create their enclosure. On the zoo map, the player can place parking lots, information centers, gift shops, animal hospitals, restaurants, and restrooms. The player must also create mammal, reptile, amphibian, and bird exhibits, and there are different animals to choose from for each of these exhibits. At each exhibit, the player must chose the number of male and female creatures, their type of habitat, and the food they'll eat. The player has limited zoo points, which define how many creatures can be placed. The player receives reports from the staff and visitors regarding the quality of the animal exhibits, and can receive advice on how to improve the condition of the zoo.
