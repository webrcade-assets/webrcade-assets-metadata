The Incredible Shrinking Adventure is a graphical text adventure for the Apple II. The player is a high-school student who has developed a formula for shrinking human beings. during their honors chemistry class. Deciding to test it on themselves, the player has shrunk to a tiny size. The player must drink the antidote prior to the science fair beginning. Unfortunately, the player still comes in 2nd place, as their classmate created a thermonuclear warhead. All commands are entered via VERB NOUN commands, and the player must use text commands to travel to different locations and solve inventory problems. Locations are graphically displayed on screen.
