Solitaire Poker is an adaptation of a video poker machine. The player starts out with placing a bet between one and seven dollars and then five cards are drawn. It's then possible to hold any number of cards before the other cards are redrawn. The objective is to have a winning hand of cards after the second drawn. There are nine different winning hands with different payouts: royal flush (500), straight flush (100), four of a kind (40), full house (10), flush (7), straight (5), three of a kind (3), two pair (2) and jacks or better (1).
