Pylon Racer is a 1-player arcade game for the Apple II. The player controls a small character who can move in all directions and fire vertically upwards and downwards. They move about a maze, and if they come in contact with a wall, they lose a life. The character battles 4 alien creatures who can fire at the player. The player advances to the next level after destroying all creatures. The player starts with 3 lives, and if all are lost, it's game over.
