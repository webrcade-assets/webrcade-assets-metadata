For the first time in an interactive game, you discover the meanings of truth, bravery and unselfishness in a whole new way. Created by Gepetto, the puppet-maker, Pinocchio is brought to life by the Blue Fairy's magic wand. Join Pinocchio and Jiminy Crickett in their journeys through Pleasure Island, a place full of games, rides and good food. Travel into the belly of Monstro the whale to save an old friend. A brilliant game with stunning animation that only Disney can provide ... an instant Classic!
