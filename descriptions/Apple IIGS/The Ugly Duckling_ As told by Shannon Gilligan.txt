The Ugly Duckling is a retelling of Hans Christian's classic fairy tale narrated by Shannon Gilligan. The ugly duckling's journeys takes her from the duck pond, to a barnyard, to a small cottage, before returning as a beautiful swan. The interactive storybook comes with animations and clickable objects. There are over 500 animations and sounds, including three songs in eight pages. New boxed versions came with a stuffed toy version of the ugly duckling.
