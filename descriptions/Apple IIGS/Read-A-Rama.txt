Read-A-Rama is an educational game for multiple systems. Designed to help young children aged 5 - 8 spell and read, there are 5 mini-games: -Read & Remember: A memory game for 1 - 4 players. -Read-A-Rama: Select which of 6 pictures represents the word shown on the screen. -Spell & Remember: The player creates word lists. -Spell-A-Rama: Spell the picture displayed on the screen. -Word-A-Rama: As the player clicks on a picture, the correctly spelled word appears.
