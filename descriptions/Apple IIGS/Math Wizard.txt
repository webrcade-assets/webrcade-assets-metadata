Math Wizard is a series of educational mini-games for multiple systems. There are 3 levels of difficulty, and the player can choose from addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division questions. -Dragon Race: Quickly answer math questions to move a dragon along the screen to reach a castle. -Elf's Equations: A math questions is displayed on the screen, and the player must select which of the fish displays the correct answer. -Troll's Toy Shop: Answer word problems, and receive a prize from a troll for correctly answered questions -Wizard Flash: A wizard displays math problems on the screen, and the player must quickly type in the answer.
