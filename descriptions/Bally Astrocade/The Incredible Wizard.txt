The Astrocade port of the Wizard of Wor arcade shooter, viewed from above. Players navigate maze-like dungeon corridors, blasting monsters that wander at varying speeds, fire darts, and can turn invisible. A radar at the bottom helps locate hidden creatures. Clearing a maze may trigger the Worluk, which doubles all scoring in the next dungeon if destroyed, or the Wizard of Wor himself for a massive point bonus. Supports two players simultaneously.
