Named after the US advertising campaign for the Datsun 280Z, 280 Zzzap has players driving up to 200 miles per hour while navigating a tricky road course at night, watching out for treacherous turns, competitors, and the ever-present time limit. Dodgem puts players in a bumper car arena where the goal is to collide with opponents while avoiding being hit yourself. It is one of the earliest games with authorized product branding.
