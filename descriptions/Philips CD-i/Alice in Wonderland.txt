Now you are Alice and Wonderland awaits you. Mad Hatter, the Cheshire Cat, the Queen of Spades, and all the Wonderland characters are there. This interactive fast-paced adventure game of the classic Alice in Wonderland excites the player with 250 amazing scenarios and 40 eccentric and rich characters. There are mushrooms, cakes and potions, all ready to eat. One will make you small as a mouse; another big as a giant! Characters appear to help (or hinder) you as you gather clues and acquire the powers needed to escape from Wonderland and return home. Rich digital color and sound add to one of the most inventive, fun CD-i programs yet.
