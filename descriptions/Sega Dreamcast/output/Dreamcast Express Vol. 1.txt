Dreamcast Express Vol. 1 is a Japanese Sega Dreamcast demo disc released as part of the Dreamcast Express series. Playable Demos - Aero Dancing featuring Blue Impulse, Buggy Heat, Pop'n Music / Videos - Geist Force (Sega), Gamera Dream Battle for the VMU (Sega), Marvel vs. Capcom: Clash of Super Heroes (Capcom), Psychic Force 2012 (Taito), Puyo Puyo 4 (Sega) / Other - Shenmue gifts, Shenmue Premiere, Making Yukawa promotion video, Blue Stinger
