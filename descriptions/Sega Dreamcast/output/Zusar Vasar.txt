Zusar Vasar (ズサーヴァサー) is a Sega Dreamcast racing game released exclusively in Japan.  Players control "chariots" pulled by mechanical animals either on land, water or in the air while racing against five other players. Two modes are available: a standard "championship" mode in which you simply race around a track, and a "battle" mode which adds weapons into the mix. A split-screen two-player mode is also supported.
