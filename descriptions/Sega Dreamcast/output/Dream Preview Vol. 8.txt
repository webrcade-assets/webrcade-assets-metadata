Dream Preview Vol. 8 (ドリーム プレピュー Vol.8) is a Japanese Sega Dreamcast promotional disc containing footage of upcoming games. Videos - Speed Devils, Super Producers: Mezase Show Biz Kai, Plus Plum, Rune Caster, Nihon Pro Mahjong Renmei Kounin: Tetsuman Menkyokaiden, Yume Baken '99 Internet, Shenmue, Revive...: Sosei, Marionette Company, Carrier, Zombie Revenge
