Dorimaga GD Vol. 3 (ドリマガＧＤ Vol.3) is a Japanese Sega Dreamcast demo disc distributed with Dreamcast Magazine issue #1999-11-26. Playable Demos - Shinkisekai Evolution 2: Tooi Yakusoku, Death Crimson 2: Meranito no Saidan, Bakuretsu Muteki Bangaioh, Vermilion Desert, Berserk, Golf Shiyouyo, Oukahouzin: Ouka Sakishi Toki
