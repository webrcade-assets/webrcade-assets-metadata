Sega Smash Pack Volume 1 was released for the Dreamcast in 2001 in North America only. It featured twelve games: -    Altered Beast -    Columns -    Golden Axe -    Phantasy Star II -    The Revenge of Shinobi -    Sega Swirl -    Shining Force -    Sonic the Hedgehog -    Streets of Rage 2 -    Vectorman -    Virtua Cop 2 -    Wrestle War
