The King of Fighters Neowave (ザ・キング・オブ・ファイターズ ネオウェーブ) is a side entry in the The King of Fighters series, released by SNK Playmore in 2004. It was the first arcade game developed by the company after finally discontinuing the Neo-Geo arcade hardware, and appeared on the Atomiswave instead. It was also ported to the Xbox and PlayStation 2. In 2020, the Atomiswave version was hacked to make it playable on Dreamcast. Sammy Atomiswave port to SEGA Dreamcast by YZB.
