Jump n' Blob (Jump and Blob) is a platformer that focuses on the Island of Malis, where a small blue creature named Blob defends the land from the Beast King and his monster army. The story involves Blob rescuing friends and the island’s home, with the journey ending in a battle against the Beast King. The island includes six areas: green valleys, windy mountains, icy caves, tropical jungles, and burning deserts.
