Dorimaga GD Vol. 11 (ドリマガＧＤ Vol.11) is a Japanese Sega Dreamcast demo disc distributed with Dreamcast Magazine issue #2001-03-30. Playable Demos - World Series Baseball 2K1, Power Jet Racing 2001 / Videos - Sakura Taisen 3: Paris wa Moeteiru ka (Opening & Special movie), Bounty Hunter Sarah: Holy Mountain no Teiou, Eldorado Gate Vol. 4, NFL 2K1, NBA 2K1, Wild Riders / VMU Data - Eternal Arcadia, Atsumare!
