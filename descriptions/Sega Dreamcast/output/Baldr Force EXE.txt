Baldr Force is a 2D action-shooter game and eroge visual novel with fast action and detailed sprite characters. The game features a world in the not-so-distant future where humans are able to dive into the network, and fight using humanoid tools called "Simulacrum". Baldr Force EXE adds, bald hell mode, a new weapon (gravity field), a super easy mode, and bald checker 1/2 data. Dreamcast and PlayStation 2 versions removed the adult only scenes.
