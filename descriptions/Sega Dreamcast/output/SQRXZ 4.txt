SQRXZ 4 is a jump-and-run game set on a mysterious island where Sqrxz, a character in need of money after planning a family, takes a job from a shady figure named Dr. Branxz. Players control Sqrxz to collect 354 ice marbles scattered across the island. The game includes traps, enemies, and hidden passages, with a particularly challenging gameplay. It features retro graphics, a top 10 highscore, and unlimited continues.
