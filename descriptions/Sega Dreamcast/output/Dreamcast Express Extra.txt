Dreamcast Express Extra is a Japanese Sega Dreamcast demo disc released as part of the Dreamcast Express series. Playable Demos: -   Cool Boarders Burrrn -   Shutokou Battle -   Sonic Adventure International -   SoulCalibur -   Chu Chu Rocket Videos: -   Shenmue -   Space Channel 5 -   DreamFlyer -   Virtua Striker 2 ver.2000.1 -   Seaman: Kindan no Pet
