Get your old Dreamcast's engine warmed up and show off your driving skills, as you take the wheel to dozens of legendary cars included in Arcade Racing Legends! At full speed, with an original soundtrack, you will try to control your vehicle on 6 varied circuits through city, desert and more. Face your friends in frenzied games thanks to the “Duel” mode or take up the challenges of the “Career” mode. Train and improve your skills to unlock additional features. Need more perfection? A Time Trial mode is here to let you know every turn on the joystick tip and take on your shadow! Developed for the 20th anniversary of the console by the publisher PixelHeart and financed via Kickstarter with more than 300 contributors. Features: • Duel 2 players • Career Mode • Time Trial Race • More than 15 vehicles • 6 Circuits • Original musics Electro, Punk/Rock Another version of the game, Arcade Racing Legends: Road Trip Edition, included an alternate cover, manual, an OST music disc, and a certificate.
