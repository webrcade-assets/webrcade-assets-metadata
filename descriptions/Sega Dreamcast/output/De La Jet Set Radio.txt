In the 21st century, the Tokyo-to city government bans kids from expressing themselves in any way. Only three things keep their spirits alive: Overdriver magnetic-motor skates, graffiti, and a pirate-radio broadcast that's called Jet Set Radio. Explore and decorate with graffiti the streets of Tokyo-to in your fight against censorship and oppression. Watch out for police who'll want to nab you for breaking the law, and rival crews that want to deface your artwork. Tag, grind, and trick to the beat in SEGA’s hit game Jet Set Radio! The De La Jet Set Radio version includes the addition of extra levels and music from the American and European versions of the game, and selection for multi-language in-game text.
