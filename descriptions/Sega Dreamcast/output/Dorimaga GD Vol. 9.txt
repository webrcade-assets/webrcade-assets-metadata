Dorimaga GD Vol. 9 (ドリマガＧＤ Vol.9) is a Japanese Sega Dreamcast demo disc distributed with Dreamcast Magazine issue #2000-12-29. Playable Demos - Never 7: The End of Infinity, Gunspike, Mars Matrix, Daytona USA 2001 / Videos - Daytona USA 2001, Ecco the Dolphin: Defender of the Future, Fighting Vipers 2, Planet Harriers
