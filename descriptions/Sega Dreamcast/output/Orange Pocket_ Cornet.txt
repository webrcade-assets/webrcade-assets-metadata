A console port of a PC (Windows) hentai visual novel Orange Pocket. While the adult content scenes were removed, rating reduced, and story adjusted to work without them, the console ports feature additional story scenario and new event CG scenes. Both console versions feature one new exclusive heroine. For PlayStation 2 that is Arika Minamoto (源 ありか) and for Dreamcast version that is Korone Kawanami (川波 ころね).
