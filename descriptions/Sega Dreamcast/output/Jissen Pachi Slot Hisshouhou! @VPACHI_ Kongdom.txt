Jissen Pachi-Slot Hisshouhou! @VPACHI: Kongdom (実戦パチスロ必勝法！＠VPACHI 〜コングダム〜) is a 2000 pachi-slot simulator for the Sega Dreamcast by Maxbet. It is a member of two pachinko/pachi-slot series, Sammy's Jissen Pachi-Slot Hisshouhou! and Daikoku's @VPACHI series, and is a simulation of a real pachi-slot machine manufactured by Yamasa called The Kongdom.
