Densha de Go! is a Japanese series of arcade train driving games. The goal for the players is to stop the train at the stations in exactly the right position while keeping up with the schedule, obeying all traffic rules, and ensuring the safety and comfort of their passengers. In order to celebrate 3000 active Densha de Go! 2 arcade units, Taito released an extended "3000 units" edition to the arcades, which was later ported to other systems. In addition to the routes of Densha de Go! 2, this edition includes the following routes:     Akita Shinkansen from Ōmagari to Morioka     Akita Shinkansen from Akita to Shin-Hanamaki     Ōu Main Line from Akita to Omagari     Tazawako Line from Tazawako to Morioka     Keihin-Tōhoku Line from Yokohama to Shinagawa     Keihin-Tōhoku Line from Yokohama to Ueno     Tōkaidō Main Line from Osaka to Kobe     Yamanote Line (Outer Circle) from Tokyo to Shinjuku
