Street Fighter III: Double Impact, known as Street Fighter III: W Impact (ストリートファイターIII Wインパクト) in Japan, is a versus fighting game developed by Capcom exclusively for the Sega Dreamcast. It is a compilation of two arcade fighters - Street Fighter III: New Generation and its update, Street Fighter III 2nd Impact: Giant Attack. As the name suggests, Street Fighter III is a sequel to Street Fighter II. The game was not released in North America. The Dreamcast would receive Street Fighter III: 3rd Strike, the second update to Street Fighter III.
