Vermilion Desert is a real-time strategy game with RPG elements. The player plans missions based on battlefield information in real-time using a map. Each unit acts automatically according to skills, situations, and directions. The player also controls a power suit unit and is creating a variety of team strategies with detailed settings, such as the range of weapons and different formations. The Silver Fox special forces are fighting against an aggressor who's invading the kingdom of Argan. Besides destroying the enemy, there are also operations like "hostage rescue", "infiltration" and "retreat operations".
