A Japan-Exclusive Turn-Based Strategy RPG based on the Japanese-Korean 3DCG anime series "Run=Dim", one of the earliest anime generated by computer graphics, set in a dystopian future where 1/10th of Japan has sunk due to global warming and an evil military organization named "JESAS" has set it's sight on controlling the future of Japan with mechas named "R.B." (Robotic Weaponry).  Being the second game in the series (the first being "The Mech Smith: Run=Dim" for the PlayStation 2) developed by Idea Factory. An English fan translation has been made recently available.
