Rune Jade is a four-player multiplayer online game for Dreamcast, courtesy of Japanese developer Hudson. Though the online service officially ended on September 30, 2001, the game let players connect and play online with fully customizable characters. Character classes included knight, ninja, necromancer, and wizard. The game can be played offline as well, as any party characters not controlled by players will be controlled by the computer. The adventure begins in the floating island country called Sonia, where demons are about to break free of the seal that keeps them under control. The game was planned for an international release but was only shipped in Japan.
