The Rumble Fish (ザ・ランブルフィッシュ) is a 2D fighting game developed by Dimps and first published by Sammy for the Atomiswave arcade platform in 2004, and was later ported by Sega to the Sony PlayStation 2 on March 17, 2005. It later received a port for the Sega Dreamcast from Dreamcast-talk user yzb in 2020. A sequel, The Rumble Fish 2, has since been developed.
