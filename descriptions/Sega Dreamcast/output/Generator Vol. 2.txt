Generator Vol. 2 is a "sequel" to Generator Vol. 1, and is a demo disc containing preview versions of various Sega Dreamcast games released in 1999 and 2000. It was only available in North America as it was bundled with Dreamcast consoles, although the majority of the demos found their way onto other demo discs across the globe. Playable Demos - Sonic Adventure, Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation, Fur Fighters, Railroad Tycoon 2, MDK 2, Tony Hawk's Pro Skater, Rayman 2: The Great Escape, Dead or Alive 2
