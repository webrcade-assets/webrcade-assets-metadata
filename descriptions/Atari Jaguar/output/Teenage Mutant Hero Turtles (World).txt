The Return Of The Awesome Foursome! 

Now two dudes or dudettes can join forces and double team Shredder and his turtle terminators in a bid to save April and Splinter. Choose which lean, green turtle you control and haul shell around this fully scrolling version of the coin-op game and save April from a burning building. Meanwhile Splinter gets kidnapped and you must trash dodgy dudes Bebop and Rocksteady to set him free.
