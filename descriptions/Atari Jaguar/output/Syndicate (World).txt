A rival Syndicate has gained full control of the population via a mind-altering techno-drug known as the CHIP.
 You are an ambitious executive in the Syndicate, hand picked to lead a team of CHIP-induced Cyborg mercenaries on a quest to infiltrate and seize opposing Syndicate territories. Your mission: Assassinate, pillage, destroy, do whatever it takes to bring down competing Agents... before they unleash their bloody assault on you!
